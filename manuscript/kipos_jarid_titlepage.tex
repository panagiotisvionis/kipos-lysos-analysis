\noindent\textbf{Ethics Approval Statement}\\
This study was conducted as an evaluation of an arts-based socio-emotional
program delivered within the context of an existing Day Care Center service.
As the study constituted an evaluation of a program that formed part of the
facility's regular provision rather than an experimental clinical intervention,
formal ethics committee review was not required under applicable national
guidelines. The study was conducted in accordance with the ethical principles
of the Declaration of Helsinki. All participants, or their legal guardians
where applicable, were informed about the evaluation purpose and provided
voluntary consent prior to inclusion. Participant anonymity and
confidentiality were maintained throughout.
