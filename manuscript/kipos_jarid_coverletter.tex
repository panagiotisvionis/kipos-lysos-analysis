\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief,}

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled \textbf{``Enhancing
Socio-Emotional Functioning of Adults with Disabilities: Evaluation of an
Experiential Intervention in a Day Care Facility''} for consideration for
publication in the \textit{Journal of Applied Research in Intellectual
Disabilities} (JARID) as an Original Article.

\textbf{What the study is about.}
This study evaluates the effectiveness of a structured, arts-based
socio-emotional empowerment program implemented over three months at a Day Care
Center serving adults with intellectual and psychosocial disabilities. The
program, entitled \textit{``The Universe Within Us,''} combined theatre, music,
movement, ceramics, and narrative dialogue within a group format, drawing on
Social and Emotional Learning (SEL), emotional intelligence theory, and
Vygotsky's socio-cultural framework.

\textbf{Design and key findings.}
Using a three-phase longitudinal design ($n = 30$ adults) and three independent
sources of evaluation---beneficiary self-reports, trainer/educator assessments,
and independent observer ratings---we found statistically significant and
practically meaningful improvements in socio-emotional functioning across all
evaluator sources (Cohen's $d$ ranging from 1.35 to 2.45, all $p < .001$).
Friedman tests confirmed significant progressive change across measurement
phases. Convergence across the three independent evaluator sources strengthens
confidence in the findings.

\textbf{Why JARID.}
We believe this manuscript is well suited for JARID for the following reasons:
\begin{itemize}
  \item It focuses on adults with intellectual and psychosocial disabilities in a
    community-based Day Care Center setting---an area currently underrepresented
    in the literature.
  \item It addresses socio-emotional empowerment, quality of life, and social
    inclusion, which are central to JARID's scope.
  \item It employs a multi-source, longitudinal evaluation design and reports
    effect sizes, contributing methodologically to the field.
  \item The documented and replicable intervention model has direct practical
    implications for disability services internationally.
\end{itemize}
