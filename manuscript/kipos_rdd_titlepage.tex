\section*{Required Statements}

\noindent\textbf{Conflict of Interest Disclosure}\\
Three of the four authors (P.V., E.C., S.C.) are affiliated with the Day Care Center
``O Kipos tis Lysous'' in which the study was conducted; E.C. and S.C. designed and
delivered the intervention under evaluation. These relationships are described in the
Researcher Positionality section of the manuscript. The authors declare no financial
conflict of interest.

\vspace{0.6cm}

\noindent\textbf{Ethics Approval Statement}\\
The study was reviewed and approved by the administration of the Day Care Center
``O Kipos tis Lysous'' (Kalamata, Greece) on 1 October 2025. As the study constituted
an evaluation of a program forming part of the facility's regular provision rather
than an experimental clinical intervention, review by an external research ethics
committee was not required under applicable national guidelines. The study was
conducted in accordance with the ethical principles of the Declaration of Helsinki.
All participants, or their legal guardians where applicable, were informed about the
purpose of the evaluation and provided voluntary consent prior to inclusion.
Participant anonymity and confidentiality were maintained throughout, and all data
were pseudonymized prior to analysis.

\vspace{0.6cm}

\noindent\textbf{Data Availability Statement}\\
The pseudonymized analysis dataset and all analysis code are publicly available at
\texttt{https://github.com/panagiotisvionis/kipos-lysos-analysis}.
The raw questionnaire export, which contains participant identifiers, is not shared.

\vspace{0.6cm}

\noindent\textbf{Funding Statement}\\
This research received no external grant funding. The study was conducted with
the institutional support of the Day Care Center ``O Kipos tis Lysous'', which provided
the research setting, access to participants, and staff time throughout the
implementation and evaluation of the program.
